GRAPH DESCRIPTIONS - ONE-LINERS
===============================================================================

1. ble2x_vs_wifi_comparison.png
   Compares power consumption and packet loss between BLE 2x repetition
   and WiFi UDP broadcast at equivalent transmission rates.

2. ble_wifi_connected_vs_disconnected.png
   Shows power overhead of maintaining WiFi connection in background while
   performing BLE advertising.

3. energy_per_byte_comparison.png
   Compares energy efficiency (mJ/byte) across BLE and WiFi modes at
   different transmission intervals.

4. power_comparison.png
   Compares power consumption between WiFi-only and hybrid BLE solution
   across different delay tolerance settings.

5. wifi_connect_disconnect_power.png
   Shows average power consumption and energy cost during WiFi connection
   and disconnection cycles.

6. wifi_idle_vs_ble_scanning.png
   Compares power consumption between idle WiFi connection and active
   BLE scanning at 1000ms intervals.

7. energy_comparison_15sec.png
   Compares total energy consumption across different communication modes
   over 15 seconds.

8. Effiwake_Power.png
   Power analysis of EffiWake system operation.
